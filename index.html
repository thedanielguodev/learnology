The problem

People can't act on what they can't understand, and many of us only find out too late.

Language barriers

Patients who don't share a language with their provider struggle to follow instructions, and translation apps give words, not understanding.

Low health literacy

Terms like "hypertension" or "dosage" are never taught in school, so people skip visits or stop treatment.

Mental health and isolation

Suicide rates have risen over the past decade despite more resources. Loneliness and poor mental health feed each other.

Hard-to-reach groups

Older adults often see doctor visits as too troublesome. Kids get no early, friendly introduction to their own bodies.

How it works

One-sentence solution: a free, multilingual, game-based platform that teaches health and medical literacy to every age.

Bite-size games

Streaks, XP and short quizzes on basic terms, illnesses and conditions.

Videos in many languages

Playful clips for kids (think GoNoodle) and more mature ones for adults.

Mental health awareness

Learn the signs, name what you feel, and know the next step.

Ready for the visit

Learn the words before the appointment so check-in is faster and less scary.
